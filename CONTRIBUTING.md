# Contributing to Candle Qi

Candle Qi is an empirical research project. Contributions should improve clarity, measurement quality, reproducibility or validation.

## Principles
- Preserve the distinction between OHLC-derived and intrabar path-derived features.
- Avoid look-ahead bias: each feature must be computable at its stated prediction time.
- State assumptions, units, data requirements and limitations.
- Treat thresholds as hypotheses until tested across assets and regimes.
- Include baselines and execution costs in strategy evaluations.
- Do not claim predictive value without out-of-sample evidence.

## Contribution format
1. Research question.
2. Operational definition and formula.
3. Required data and sampling assumptions.
4. Expected failure modes.
5. Test design and baseline comparison.
6. Results, including negative or inconclusive results.
