# References and related work

This is a starting bibliography, not evidence that Candle Qi itself works. Traditional candlestick pattern analysis, volatility dynamics and order-flow research are adjacent fields; the project's intrabar behavioural measurements remain to be developed and tested.

## Technical analysis and chart-pattern measurement

**Lo, A. W., Mamaysky, H., & Wang, J. (2000).** “Foundations of Technical Analysis: Computational Algorithms, Statistical Inference, and Empirical Implementation.” *The Journal of Finance*, 55(4), 1705–1765.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00265
- Relevance: systematic, algorithmic recognition of technical patterns and statistical evaluation rather than visual interpretation alone.

## Order flow and short-term price changes

**Cont, R., Kukanov, A., & Stoikov, S. (2014).** “The Price Impact of Order Book Events.” *Journal of Financial Econometrics*, 12(1), 47–88.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/jjfinec/nbt003
- Relevance: order-flow imbalance and short-horizon price impact. This is adjacent to, but not the same as, price-only directional persistence.

## Volatility and market dynamics

**Engle, R. F. (1982).** “Autoregressive Conditional Heteroscedasticity with Estimates of the Variance of United Kingdom Inflation.” *Econometrica*, 50(4), 987–1007.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.2307/1912773
- Relevance: foundational conditional-volatility modelling.

**Bollerslev, T. (1986).** “Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity.” *Journal of Econometrics*, 31(3), 307–327.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-4076(86)90063-1
- Relevance: volatility persistence and clustering; useful baseline family when evaluating whether Candle Qi adds information.

## Traditional candlestick taxonomy

Candlestick patterns such as doji, marubozu, spinning top, hammer, shooting star and engulfing are widely described in technical-analysis literature. The project treats their geometry as descriptive labels, not proven causal signals. Any specific pattern claim should be tested against a clearly defined market, timeframe, sample and execution model.

## Research discipline

Do not cite adjacent literature as validation of Candle Qi. Each proposed feature and predictive claim requires its own test.
